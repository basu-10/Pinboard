Pinboard

An infinite, pannable board for your notes and images — everything stays in your browser, no account and no server. This is a quick tour of how it feels to use, rendered with a live mock of the app itself.

The surface

An infinite canvas you can roam

The board is one endless dotted grid. Drag empty space to pan anywhere, or use the corner joystick when pointer drags are awkward. Arrow keys nudge by a cell, and the camera follows your content as it grows.

Pins always snap to whole cells, so nothing ever floats at a fractional offset — the layout stays tidy no matter how far you wander.

Capture

Hover a cell, drop in a pin

Hover an empty cell and it lights up with an inset accent ring — the add options live right inside that cell, so the action reads as placing content where you're looking.

Choose + Note, + Image, or Paste from the clipboard. A single click on the cell itself adds a note without aiming for a button. You can also drop files straight from your computer.

Arrange

Drag to reorganize, snapped to the grid

Press and drag any pin to move it. A live ghost shows exactly where it will land — green when the target cell is free, marked when it's taken.

Everything snaps to whole cells, so pins never overlap. Release to commit; the move is saved and fully undoable.

Shape

Right-click to resize by cells

Right-click a pin to reveal resize handles on its edges and corner. Drag a handle and the pin grows or shrinks by whole grid cells, never colliding with its neighbors.

The new span is written the moment you let go — great for giving an image room or stretching a note across a column.

Organize

Color pins to group at a glance

Give any pin an accent from three palettes of ten presets — Soft, Vivid, and Earth — or clear it back to neutral. A colored pin shows a tinted card and a colored ring, so categories read instantly across the board.

Start fast

Begin from a template

Don't start from a blank grid. A template picker offers Kanban, weekly planners, roadmaps, brainstorm webs, and more — grouped into Planning, Work, Creative, and Personal tabs.

Each layout seeds the board with pre-placed, color-coded pins, so the structure is already there to fill in.

Find anything

Search and a self-aware minimap

The magnifier slides out a search panel: pick a scope (title, body, or both) and matches list with the term highlighted and the pin's grid position. Click one to fly the camera straight to it.

Nearby pins cluster into groups. The minimap shows every group plus your viewport; jump between clusters, or zoom-to-fit to bring all content home.

Keep it

Save boards, back them up

Name the board and hit save to snapshot it into your Library — a cover preview, pin counts, and the saved date. Open a board to load it back, or delete it.

For safekeeping, export a board as a single JSON backup, import it anywhere, or flatten it to a zip of plain .txt and image files.
